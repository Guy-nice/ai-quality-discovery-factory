# Stakeholder Questionnaire

**Version:** v0 | **Date:** 2026-10-22 | **Current round:** 1

Rules: ask the most important questions first. Do not ask what evidence can answer. Each question states why it matters and which decision it enables.

## Round 1 - Charter alignment (sent 2026-10-22)

### Blocking (Gate 0 / Gate 1)

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Decision enabled | Ask | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which product, domain, monolith, and services are in scope? What is explicitly out? | Without a boundary the research has no limit. | Charter scope | Sponsor / Guy | Open |
| Q2 | Who is the sponsor and decision owner? What decision will they make with the output, and by when? | Output format and depth depend on the decision. | Success criteria, timeline | Guy | Open |
| Q3 | What signal started this concern (incident, metric, executive remark, escalation)? When? | First traceable signal for the reasoning chain. | Problem statement v1 | Sponsor | Open |
| Q4 | What data may this repository hold (Jira keys, incident summaries, customer names, code excerpts)? | Compliance gate for all evidence capture. | Evidence handling rule | Security / Guy | Open |
| Q5 | May the research run read-only queries in Jira and Confluence? Which project and space keys? | Gate 1 needs real access checks. | Access inventory | Guy | Open |
